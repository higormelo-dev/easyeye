## 1.3.0 — October 18, 2026

Commercial management: insurance contracts, card brand, support at hand and this page.

- **Contract validity on insurance plans.** In Settings → Insurance plans, each of the clinic's plans gets a contract start and end date (optional). The dashboard of administrators and finance warns about contracts that have expired and those expiring in the next 30 days. It is a notice only: it does not block care or TISS billing.
- **Card brand on the cash flow.** When you record a payment by credit, credit + cash or debit + cash in the Cash flow, you can enter the card brand (Visa, Mastercard, Elo, American Express, Hipercard, Diners Club or Other). It shows in the cash flow table and in the report. The cash flow CSV and Excel exports now also include the **Payment method** and **Card brand** columns, at the end.
- **Support in one click.** An icon at the top of every panel screen opens the official support WhatsApp with the message already filled in (or e-mail, when WhatsApp is not configured).
- **What's new.** This page gathers what changed in each version. The **What's new** menu item shows a "new" mark until you open it and shows it again when a new version is released.

## 1.2.0 — October 18, 2026

Subscription invoices.

- **Electronic service invoice (NFS-e) for your subscription.** EasyEye now issues the electronic service invoice (NFS-e) for the clinic's subscription. Under **My subscription**, administrators, finance and account owners can download the invoice XML and, if it is cancelled, the cancellation XML. Each clinic only sees its own invoices.

## 1.1.0 — October 16, 2026

Exam reports and sign-up payment.

- **Voice dictation in reports.** The report text can be dictated and transcribed by the server. The feature is off by default and enabled by EasyEye; each dictation is recorded in the audit trail.
- **Several reports per printed sheet.** Choose how many exam reports fit on each printed sheet.
- **Returning a report to the technician.** The doctor can send a report back to the technician with a reason, and the technician is notified.
- **Sign-up with the owner's CPF or CNPJ.** "Get started" asks for the document and for acceptance of the terms and privacy policy, and payment can be made right away by Pix, bank slip or card.
